# Omsetra

### Start small. Start free. Bring clarity to business payments.

An early-access reconciliation and collections workspace for Indian founders, small businesses and accountants.

## What you can explore

- Three guided demo journeys with fictional records and no signup required.
- Guided workspace onboarding for founders, existing businesses and accountants.
- Bank statement CSV, Excel and text-based PDF import previews, with records checked before saving. Extraction quality depends on the source layout; scanned PDFs need additional handling.
- Tally Sales Register imports and reconciliation review workflows.
- Matching suggestions and probable TDS flags for human review. A suggestion is not proof of a tax deduction or a filing.
- Private business dashboards, separate from public demo data.

## Omsetra and payment gateways

Omsetra focuses on the work around business payments: organizing records, reviewing reconciliation and preparing collections workflows. Razorpay provides payment-gateway services; Omsetra is not currently a replacement for processing or settling payments.

The planned integration connects each business's own merchant account through official APIs after the required approvals. Provider onboarding, charges and terms apply separately. The free Omsetra experience does not imply free payment processing.

Omsetra is an independent product. No Razorpay endorsement, partnership approval or regulatory authorization is claimed.
