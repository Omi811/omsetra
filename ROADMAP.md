# Omsetra roadmap

Status: early access. Priorities may change with user feedback; these are not delivery commitments.

## Explore today

- Three fictional demo journeys before signup.
- Private workspace onboarding for founders, businesses and accountants.
- Preview imported records before saving and review reconciliation suggestions.

## Before payment activation

- Complete provider partner approval and merchant OAuth configuration.
- Verify per-business authorization, encrypted merchant credentials and webhook handling.
- Test isolation, failed payments, refunds and reconciliation across separate merchants.
- Complete launch review and publish real support and privacy-request contact details.
- Enable live processing only after explicit launch authorization. Keep it disabled until then.

## Authentication preparation

- Prepare Supabase Auth using the owner's selected existing project.
- Resolve hosting support for external identities while preserving existing workspace ownership.
- Configure Google, Apple and Microsoft developer applications and production email delivery.
- Verify account linking and access controls before changing the live login experience.

## Future workflows

- Broader bank-layout coverage and scanned-document extraction.
- Zoho Books sync, richer Tally workflows and merchant payment-link integration.
- Opt-in WhatsApp collection reminders through official APIs.
- Better reconciliation exceptions, reporting and accountant workflows.

Suggest priorities through public issues using fictional data only.
