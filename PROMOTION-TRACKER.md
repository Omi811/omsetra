## Outreach boundaries

- Use Omsetra branding. No fake founder identities, testimonials, users, revenue or approval claims.
- Do not publish private personal contact details from provider dashboards.
- Do not call Omsetra a functioning Razorpay replacement or advertise live UPI collection.
- Do not submit under personal accounts without the owner's choice of account.
- No CAPTCHA bypasses, duplicate promotional posts, bought engagement or unsolicited bulk messaging.

## Measure outcomes

Use channel-tagged demo links, for example `?utm_source=launchingnext&utm_medium=directory&utm_campaign=free_demo`. A tagged URL is not analytics implementation. Measure referred demo visits and useful feedback when analytics exists; do not claim traffic or conversions without evidence.
