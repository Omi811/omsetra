## Outreach boundaries

- Use Omsetra branding. No fake founder identities, testimonials, users, revenue or approval claims.
- Do not publish private personal contact details from provider dashboards.
- Do not call Omsetra a functioning Razorpay replacement or advertise live UPI collection.

- No CAPTCHA bypasses, duplicate promotional posts, bought engagement or unsolicited bulk messaging.

## Measure outcomes
