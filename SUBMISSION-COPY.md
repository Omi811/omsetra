## Directory description

Omsetra helps Indian founders, small businesses and accountants bring invoices and bank records into a clearer workflow. Explore three interactive demo journeys with fictional data before creating an account: launch a first product, review bank-to-invoice reconciliation, or try client-account review.

The early-access application includes guided private workspace onboarding, bank statement import previews, Tally Sales Register imports, matching suggestions and reviewed reconciliation workflows. Probable TDS differences are flags for human review, not tax verification.

Starter begins at ₹0 platform subscription with no card needed to explore the demo.
