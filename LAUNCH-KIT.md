## 60-second demo script

1. Open the demo and explain that every record is fictional.
2. Choose the founder journey to show the simplest starting point.
3. Switch to the existing-business journey and show a reconciliation exception.
4. Show the accountant journey and explain the private workspace boundary.
5. Close with the free demo link and invite product feedback.
6. State clearly that live payments remain disabled.

## Promotion sequence

1. Publish the GitHub product page and use the demo as the primary call to action.
2. Share the LinkedIn copy from the founder's account after checking its current feature status.
3. Publish a short screen recording using fictional demo records only.
4. Participate in relevant founder and accountant communities where promotion is permitted. Ask for workflow feedback; avoid unsolicited bulk messages.
5. Track demo visits, journey completion, signup and onboarding completion with consent-appropriate analytics. Do not invent adoption figures or testimonials.

## Claims to avoid

Do not describe Omsetra as a licensed payment aggregator, approved Razorpay partner, production-ready gateway replacement, fee-free payment processor or provider of every Razorpay feature. Do not promise tax compliance, guaranteed matches or bank-grade certification without evidence. Do not advertise planned integrations as live.
